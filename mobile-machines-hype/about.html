About the project

One phone. A share of something real.

Mobile Machines is a 4,444-supply collection where every phone is a working signal, not just a picture. Once minted, each one starts earning a share of real tokenized stock — paid out on a recurring cycle to the phones that stay active. This page tracks where the project actually is right now.

How the earnings work

Every cycle, a share of real tokenized stock is paid out to the top 100 active phones — ranked by how much weight each phone is carrying. Spinning here doesn't touch any of that; it only ever reserves a spot on the guaranteed-mint roster, decided entirely by our server. Nothing to buy, no wallet connection needed until you actually land a spot.
